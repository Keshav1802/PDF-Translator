Privacy Policy for PDF Translator

Last Updated: October 9, 2026
Developer: Keshav • Contact: keshav.dev.contact@gmail.com

1. Overview

PDF Translator is committed to protecting your privacy. The extension operates with a zero-telemetry, client-side architecture: all PDF parsing, vector coordinate calculation, and document rendering take place directly inside your browser. We operate zero user tracking servers, collect no personal identities, and never sell or monetize user data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Browsing Activity: We do not track, log, or monitor your browsing history or visited sites.
  • Financial Details: No payment details or transaction data are ever handled.
  • Location Data: We do not collect GPS, device telemetry, or IP-based location data.
  • Third-Party Analytics: No tracking SDKs (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) are embedded.

3. How Data Is Handled

A. Local In-Memory PDF Processing

When you view or translate a PDF, Mozilla's open-source PDF.js engine renders vector graphics, fonts, and layouts entirely within your browser's local memory. No PDF documents are ever uploaded to any developer-owned server.

B. Translation Processing

Selected text blocks from the active document are sent directly to the translation service chosen by the user (such as Google Translate or user-configured Gemini API) via encrypted HTTPS. This text is transmitted solely to perform the real-time translation and is never saved or logged by the extension.

C. Local Preferences Storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.local exclusively on your device to save your UI preferences (target language, split-view mode, and optional user-entered API keys). This data stays 100% on your local machine.

4. Permissions Disclosure

Permission Specific Purpose
storage Saves language preference and viewer settings locally on your machine.
activeTab Checks if the current tab is displaying a PDF when the extension icon is clicked.
contextMenus Provides a right-click "Translate this PDF" shortcut on web links.
tabs Opens the dual-pane viewer in a new tab and inspects URL format.
host_permissions Allows fetching remote PDFs chosen by the user and connecting to translation APIs.

5. Security

All network communications use industry-standard HTTPS encryption. The extension loads zero remote executable scripts in strict compliance with Chrome Web Store Manifest V3 guidelines.
